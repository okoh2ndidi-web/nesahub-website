START WITH YOUR NEEDS

Think beyond the room.

Your accommodation

Consider whether a room, self-contained accommodation, hostel or lodge fits your needs. These are accommodation types, not confirmed inventory.

Your location

Consider the journey to your campus, transport and everyday amenities. Ask about current supported locations.

Your budget

Ask for the rent, additional charges, payment period and terms before committing.

BEFORE AN ENQUIRY

Check the details that matter.

  1. Review the information

    Look for clear photos, property details, location and availability.

  2. Ask about a viewing

    Confirm the contact person and viewing arrangements before travelling.

  3. Understand the agreement

    Clarify charges, terms and what is included before proceeding.
