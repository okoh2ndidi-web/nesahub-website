ABOUT NESAHub

Built around property.
Connected around people.

NESAHub connects property and home services across Nigeria, bringing workmen, materials, housing and property management together.

WHY NESAHub

Property needs rarely stop at one service.

A home may need a repair. A repair may need materials. A vacant property may need a tenant. A landlord needs records that make sense.

NESAHub brings these needs into one connected vision: workmen, building-material traders, housing discovery and property management.

The platform is for homeowners and clients, students and housing seekers, landlords and agents, artisans and suppliers.
