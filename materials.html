WHAT YOU MAY NEED

INTENDED SERVICE JOURNEY

A quotation should make the details clear.

  1. Request

    Specify the materials, quantities, location and relevant requirements.

  2. Quotation

    Review the supplier’s description, price, quantities and proposed delivery terms.

  3. Acceptance and order

    Confirm what you accept, how payment works and who is responsible for delivery.

  4. Delivery and records

    Check delivered items against the agreement and keep evidence or records where applicable.

BEFORE YOU AGREE

Check more than the headline price.

The materials

Confirm specification, unit, quantity and whether substitutions are acceptable.

The delivery

Clarify timing, location, transport cost and responsibility for shortages or damage.
