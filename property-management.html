THE EVERYDAY ESSENTIALS

A clearer view of your records.

Properties & dashboard

Organise property information and portfolio summaries, depending on the records available.

Tenants & leases

Maintain tenant details, occupancy, lease dates, rental arrangements and renewal history.

Rent, receipts & ledger

Tools cover payment recording, receipts and financial history. Confirm how receipts are shared and how your rental arrangement is handled before relying on the records.

Expenses & performance

Understand income alongside repair, upkeep and other property expenses. This is not a promise of formal accounting functionality.

BEYOND RENT

Keep the wider property history.

Inspections

Organise inspection dates, notes, condition information and follow-up actions where supported.

Notices

Keep records of tenancy communications. Generated documents should not be treated as legal advice.

Documents

Property and tenancy documents need private handling. Confirm the supported document service before sharing personal records.

Reports & reminders

Reporting and reminder tools are still developing. Confirm which reports are available; live email or WhatsApp reminder delivery is not offered through this website.

WHEN A PROPERTY BECOMES VACANT

From management to housing.

  1. Record the change

    Keep the tenancy and vacancy information accurate.

  2. Prepare the listing

    Review the property details, images, price and availability.

  3. Submit for approval

    Marketplace publication is subject to the applicable approval process; it is not automatic.

Before you start managing
