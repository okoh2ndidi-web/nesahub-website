HOW IT WORKS

Four services.
Four clear journeys.

Choose the process relevant to you. Each service has its own responsibilities and next steps.

01 · WORKMEN

From a request to a reviewed job.

  1. Describe

    Share the job, location, evidence where applicable and budget range.

  2. Agree

    Clarify scope, costing, payment arrangements and assignment.

  3. Follow through

    Track progress where supported and clarify completion confirmation.

Read about workmen ↗

02 · MATERIALS & TRADERS

From specification to delivery.

  1. Specify

    Describe the materials, quantities and destination.

  2. Review and accept

    Check quotation details and agree order, payment and delivery terms.

  3. Check delivery

    Compare delivered items with the agreement and keep appropriate records.

Read about materials ↗

03 · HOUSING

From discovery to an enquiry.

  1. Explore

    Consider rent, sale or student accommodation and relevant property information.

  2. Check

    Confirm availability, price, location and the contact’s role.

  3. Enquire

    Discuss the property or arrange a viewing through a supported destination.

Read about housing ↗

04 · PROPERTY MANAGEMENT

From scattered information to records.

  1. Organise

    Add or maintain the property and tenancy information in the existing application where supported.

  2. Record

    Maintain payments, receipts, expenses and other relevant records.

  3. Review

    Follow up on upcoming rent, vacancies and property matters using verified tools.
