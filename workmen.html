THE PROCESS

A managed approach to getting work done.

  1. Describe the job

    Share the location, problem, urgency and budget range. Supporting evidence can help explain the work.

  2. Clarify scope and costing

    Confirm what is included, any assessment needed, the amount and payment arrangements before work begins.

  3. Assignment and progress

    The intended service process includes operator oversight, a suitable assignment and progress records.

  4. Completion and follow-up

    Review the work and keep relevant evidence. Clarify how your completion confirmation will be recorded.
