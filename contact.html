FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few useful answers.

What does NESAHub cover?

Workmen, building materials and traders, housing, and property management. Each service page explains its purpose and current limitations.

Can I pay or complete a booking on these pages?

These pages do not process payments or confirm bookings. The existing homepage form sends a job request through its existing provider.

Does a listed profile mean it is verified?

No. Registration or listing is distinct from verification. Ask what checks apply and have been completed.

Where does NESAHub operate?

NESAHub’s current scope is Nigeria. Share your city, state and the service you need so we can confirm local availability.

Where is your office located?

Opposite UNIBEN, 19th Street, 3 Omoruyi Street, Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria. Contact NESAHub to confirm opening hours before visiting.

Where do professionals register?

The Professionals page explains participation paths. Online professional registration is not available through this website yet. Contact NESAHub for current information.

Are there live property listings here?

No. The housing pages currently explain the service. They do not display live listings or confirm availability.
