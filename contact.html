WHY NESAHub

Property needs rarely stop at one service.

The platform is for homeowners and clients, students and housing seekers, landlords and agents, artisans and suppliers.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few useful answers.

What does NESAHub cover?

Workmen, Building Materials, Housing and Property Management. Phase 1 offers information and general enquiries only; application workflows remain under development and security qualification.

Can I pay or complete a booking on these pages?

These pages do not process payments or confirm bookings. Opening WhatsApp does not confirm a booking, quotation, assignment or payment.

Does a listed profile mean it is verified?

No. Registration or listing is distinct from verification. Ask what checks apply and have been completed.

Where does NESAHub operate?

Ask general questions about current coverage in Nigeria, including Benin City and student accommodation around UNIBEN, Ugbowo and Ekosodin. These pages do not promise available inventory or operational services.

Where is your office located?

Opposite UNIBEN, 19th Street, 3 Omoruyi Street, Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria. Contact NESAHub to confirm opening hours before visiting.

Can professionals join through these pages?

No. Phase 1 provides general information about future participation. It does not register professionals, activate accounts, assign work or arrange customer introductions.

Are there live property listings here?

No. Read our Housing information and Student Housing information, or ask general coverage questions. NESAHub general WhatsApp is for company enquiries, not property owner contact. These pages do not arrange viewings or bookings.
